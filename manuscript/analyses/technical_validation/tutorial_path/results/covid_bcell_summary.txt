Threadfin summary
=================
Cells with a BCR: 39,503 of 61,423, in 38,059 clones (588 clones have two or more cells and are profiled).

1. Are cells of one clone more alike than cells of different clones? (clonal coherence)
   Clone identity explains 40.0% of the variance of cell states (the expression embedding); with clone labels shuffled within 'donor x sample', 5.2% (p = 0.00498, 200 permutations): an excess of 34.8 percentage points.
   Profiles are shrunk towards the average profile, so a clone's (smoothed) profile reaches reliability 0.5 at 2 cells; no clone is filtered out on reliability.

2. Which clones behave alike? (clonal programmes)
   No distinct programmes: no split of the 588 profiled clones into separate groups was significant (split test p = 0.084).

3. Do your labels explain how clones differ? (clone-level tests)
   Labels are shuffled among clones of the same donor and of similar size (2, 3-4, 5-9 or >= 10 cells), because small clones' profiles are pulled towards the average profile: an effect below cannot be explained by clone size alone.
   antigen_call explains clone states: 6.8% of clone-profile variance vs 4.2% for shuffled labels (p = 0.0005, 484 clones; 104 clones without a majority level left out).
   isotype explains clone states: 10.7% of clone-profile variance vs 3.7% for shuffled labels (p = 0.0005, 510 clones; 78 clones without a majority level left out).
