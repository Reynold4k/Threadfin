Cells with a BCR: 123,369 of 205,630, in 87,396 clones (6,124 clones have two or more cells and are profiled).

1. Are cells of one clone more alike than cells of different clones? (clonal coherence)
   Clone identity explains 15.8% of the variance of cell states (the expression embedding); with clone labels shuffled within 'sample', 4.3% (p = 0.00498, 200 permutations): an excess of 11.5 percentage points.
   Profiles are shrunk towards the average profile, so a clone's (smoothed) profile reaches reliability 0.5 at 6 cells; no clone is filtered out on reliability.

   P1: 3,693 clones, 23,809 cells, stability 0.79
   P2: 2,431 clones, 18,288 cells, stability 0.54  [unstable - do not interpret]

3. Do your labels explain how clones differ? (clone-level tests)

   gfp explains clone states: 10.8% of clone-profile variance vs 0.2% for shuffled labels (p = 0.0005, 5009 clones; 799 clones without a majority level left out).
