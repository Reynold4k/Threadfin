Threadfin summary
=================
Cells with a BCR: 2,115 of 4,121, in 1,367 clones (204 clones have two or more cells and are profiled).

1. Are cells of one clone more alike than cells of different clones? (clonal coherence)
   Clone identity explains 16.2% of the variance of cell states (the expression embedding); with clone labels shuffled within 'sample', 5.1% (p = 0.00498, 200 permutations): an excess of 11.1 percentage points.
   Profiles are shrunk towards the average profile, so a clone's (smoothed) profile reaches reliability 0.5 at 4 cells; no clone is filtered out on reliability.

2. Which clones behave alike? (clonal programmes)
   No distinct programmes: no split of the 204 profiled clones into separate groups was significant (split test p = 0.11).

3. Do your labels explain how clones differ? (clone-level tests)
   Labels are shuffled among clones of the same donor and of similar size (2, 3-4, 5-9 or >= 10 cells), because small clones' profiles are pulled towards the average profile: an effect below cannot be explained by clone size alone.
   ha_binding does not significantly explain clone states: 1.7% of clone-profile variance vs 1.9% for shuffled labels (p = 0.516, 151 clones; 53 clones without a majority level left out).
   tissue explains clone states: 26.2% of clone-profile variance vs 1.6% for shuffled labels (p = 0.0005, 194 clones; 10 clones without a majority level left out).
      This label is fixed within each sample: the comparison is between samples (n = 15), not between independent clones.
   isotype explains clone states: 5.1% of clone-profile variance vs 0.9% for shuffled labels (p = 0.001, 199 clones; 5 clones without a majority level left out).

4. Do clones keep their state over 'tissue'? (clonal memory; levels in the order Lung -> medLN)
   26 clones seen at >= 2 levels: memory index -0.07 [-0.44, 0.22], p = 0.776 (1 = clones stay as distinct as they were, 0 = no more similar to their earlier self than to another clone of its donor seen at the same levels; sampling noise can put it outside 0-1).
