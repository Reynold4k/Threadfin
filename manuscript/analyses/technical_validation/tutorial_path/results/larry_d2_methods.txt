Clonal analyses used Threadfin 4.5.0 (Python 3.11.17, scanpy 1.11.5, anndata 0.12.19). Clone identities were taken from adata.obs['clone_id']. 4,638 of 28,249 cells belonged to 2,672 clones, 1,401 of them with two or more cells. Cell states were the coordinates of the embedding 'X_published'. Each cell's coordinates were centred on the mean of its sampling context ('sample'). Each cell was averaged with its 15 nearest neighbours and mapped to 256 random Fourier features of a Gaussian kernel (bandwidth 13.2, the median distance between cells; ref. 1); every clone with at least 2 cells was described by the mean of its cells' features (kernel mean embedding; ref. 2), shrunk towards the context average the more, the fewer cells it has (best linear unbiased prediction under a random-effects model; ref. 3), and the profiles were reduced to 30 principal components. Clonal coherence was the intraclass correlation of cell states within clones (the share of variance explained by clone, on unsmoothed, context-centred coordinates), tested against 200 permutations of clone labels within 'sample' (observed 0.680, shuffled 0.010, P = 0.005, the smallest possible with this number of permutations). The clone map is a UMAP (ref. 4) of the clone profiles with n_neighbors 100 and min_dist 0.8, chosen by Threadfin's layout search; it is a display, and no statistic depends on it. Clonal programmes were Leiden communities (ref. 5) of the 15-nearest-neighbour graph of clone profiles (edges weighted by the clones' reliabilities), at the resolution chosen for bootstrap stability; every split was tested against a single Gaussian group (alpha 0.05) and splits that failed were merged; stability was the mean Jaccard overlap with the best-matching group over 30 bootstrap replicates (ref. 6). This gave 4 programmes.
