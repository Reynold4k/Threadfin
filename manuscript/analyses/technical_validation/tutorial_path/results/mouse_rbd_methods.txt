Clonal analyses used Threadfin 4.5.0 (Python 3.11.17, scanpy 1.11.5, anndata 0.12.19). Clones were called with Dandelion 0.5.7 (ref. 1) within each donor ('donor'), from productive contigs, requiring the same V and J genes and junction length on the heavy and light chains and a junction amino-acid identity of at least 0.85. Cells without a heavy chain were not assigned to a clone. Python's hash seed was fixed (0) so that clone calls are reproducible. 16,667 of 36,188 cells belonged to 8,736 clones, 1,881 of them with two or more cells. Counts were normalised to 10,000 per cell and log-transformed; 3,000 highly variable genes (Seurat flavour) were selected after excluding immunoglobulin and T-cell receptor genes, scaled and reduced to 30 principal components. Each cell's coordinates were centred on the mean of its sampling context ('donor'). Each cell was averaged with its 15 nearest neighbours and mapped to 256 random Fourier features of a Gaussian kernel (bandwidth 20, the median distance between cells; ref. 2); every clone with at least 2 cells was described by the mean of its cells' features (kernel mean embedding; ref. 3), shrunk towards the context average the more, the fewer cells it has (best linear unbiased prediction under a random-effects model; ref. 4), and the profiles were reduced to 30 principal components. Clonal coherence was the intraclass correlation of cell states within clones (the share of variance explained by clone, on unsmoothed, context-centred coordinates), tested against 200 permutations of clone labels within 'donor' (observed 0.169, shuffled 0.007, P = 0.005, the smallest possible with this number of permutations). The clone map is a UMAP (ref. 5) of the clone profiles with n_neighbors 10 and min_dist 0.95, chosen by Threadfin's layout search; it is a display, and no statistic depends on it. Clonal programmes were Leiden communities (ref. 6) of the 15-nearest-neighbour graph of clone profiles (edges weighted by the clones' reliabilities), at the resolution chosen for bootstrap stability; every split was tested against a single Gaussian group (alpha 0.05) and splits that failed were merged; stability was the mean Jaccard overlap with the best-matching group over 30 bootstrap replicates (ref. 7). This gave 3 programmes. For 'rbd_bait', 'division_gate', 'zone_gate', 'isotype', the share of the variance between clone profiles explained by the clone-level label (as in PERMANOVA; ref. 8) was tested with 2,000 permutations of the label among clones of the same 'donor'. Clonal memory over 'division_gate' compared the change of each clone's profile between levels, corrected for sampling, with the distance to a random clone of the same donor.
