Cells with a TCR: 2,793 of 3,000, in 2,373 clones (78 clones have >= 3 cells).

1. Does clone identity shape cell state? (clonal coherence)
   Clone identity explains 26.5% of transcriptional variance within 'sample' strata; shuffled clones explain 3.0% (p = 0.00498, 200 permutations).

2. Which clones behave alike? (clonal programmes)
   No distinct programmes: the 191 reliable clones differ, but along a continuum rather than in separate groups (split test p = 0.96).

3. Do your labels explain how clones differ? (clone-level tests)
